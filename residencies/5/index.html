Molly Nilsson - Sólo Paraíso
03 Feb 14
Sólo Paraíso - Cover Art by Máximo Pedraza
  • Castellano
  • English

Molly Nilsson recorded "Sólo Paraíso" during her two-month residency at Bellefour in February and March 2014, and presented the songs during a farewell concert on the terrace of Otero. She also released multiple videos after her stay - both of footage she recorded in Buenos Aires, others in collaboration with graw böckler.

The videos can be seen on Molly's Youtube & Vimeo pages, and on Graw Böckler's site below:
