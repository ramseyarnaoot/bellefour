Elisabeth Wood presented "Writing/Meditation as Noise Intervention", a daily open-hour writing exercise held at Bellefour from March 8 through April 5, 2015. Each day, a rotating curator-of-the-day presented a topic — communicated through a story, image, phrase, or single word — and participants wrote together in disciplined silence or with a soundtrack, sharing a creative and solitary state in a spontaneous community setting.

Writing was not shared during the sessions; instead, participants were invited to contribute fragments to a compiled collection, published as Anonymous Collection of an Open Hour.

During her residency, Elisabeth also recorded a set of songs inspired by her time in Buenos Aires.
