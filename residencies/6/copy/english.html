Elisabeth Wood presented "Writing/Meditation as Noise Intervention", a daily open-hour writing exercise held at Bellefour from March 8 through April 5, 2015. Each day, a rotating curator-of-the-day presented a topic — communicated through a story, image, phrase, or single word — and participants wrote together in disciplined silence or with a soundtrack, sharing a creative and solitary state in a spontaneous community setting.

Writing was not shared during the sessions; instead, participants were invited to contribute fragments to a compiled collection, published as Anonymous Collection of an Open Hour. The Buenos Aires session was the first installment of what became The Anonymous Writing Group (TAWG), an ongoing project that has since produced collections in Berlin, internationally by email, and beyond.

During her residency, Elisabeth also recorded a set of songs during her time in Buenos Aires.
