Feel like Home
29 Feb 16
  • Castellano
  • English
How does it feel to enter a room that is tailored so specifically for you that it becomes disconcerting? How does it feel to experience this while you re travelling and trying to disconnect from your everyday life ? Could it be the blueprint for a new kind of futuristic hotel ? Or is it a traveler’s nightmare ? Based on the information provided by our guests’ social network profiles, we deliver them a personalized room in order for them to « feel like home ».
